Our Friendship Starts from Grade 10

Chapter One

Dear Cendy,

I don't even know who I'm writing to anymore. Maybe I'm writing to myself. Maybe to the memories I'm afraid to forget.

My name is Emma, and if there's one thing I've learned from high school, it's that feelings are never simple. Especially when it comes to the people you care about the most.

There's this girl, Cendy. She's not just my classmate; she's my person. The one who knows when I'm lying about being "fine," the one who laughs too loudly at my jokes, and the one who somehow makes everything feel less heavy.

But even the strongest friendships have their quiet complications.

Cendy likes to pretend she doesn't believe in love, but I've seen the way her eyes change. First, it was Liam, then somehow... Ressie. And maybe she doesn't even realize it yet, but her heart is somewhere in between. (By the way, she has a boyfriend.)

And me? I wish my story were less obvious. But it isn't.

Alex. His name sits in my chest like something permanent.

I don't remember when it started, and I don't want to.

The hardest part about all of this isn't the feelings. It's the silence around them. The things we don't say. The glances we pretend not to notice. The moments that mean everything but pass like they meant nothing at all.

High school was supposed to be simple: just classes, exams, and growing up.

But no one tells you about this part... the part where your heart gets involved, where friendships feel like they could either last forever or fall apart at any second.

Maybe one day we'll look back at all of this and laugh. Maybe we'll remember it as the time everything changed us.

I don't know how our story ends. I don't know who ends up with whom.

But I do know this: some stories don't end when we want them to. Some feelings don't fade how we expect them to.

And maybe... this is just the beginning of something that will one day break us... or shape us into who we're meant to be.

Emma.
